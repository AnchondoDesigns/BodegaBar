№ 04 — ART

The walls are part of the menu.

Every few months, a local artist takes over the bar — murals, prints, photography. Atlanta has one of the strongest street-art lineages in the country, and this corner intends to hang it. Cuisine & art, intertwined — not a tagline, a rotation.
